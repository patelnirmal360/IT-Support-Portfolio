- Step 1: Go to users and All users then select the employee you want to update information in my case is David.

- Step 3: Edit the required properties under the job information
- Step 4: click save

- Step 2: Now select the member (David) so on top navigation bar the remove button will be active and click remove and click YES. And refresh and check member is removed or not
## Add new group memberships.

  - Step 2: Click Add members then search for member you want to add (David) and click select
